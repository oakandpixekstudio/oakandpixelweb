Questions

Answers before you ask

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply personally.

How much does a website cost?

Signature is R12,500 setup plus R1,800 per month. Growth is R25,000 setup plus R3,200 per month. Premium starts from R55,000 setup plus R6,500 per month and is quoted to your scope. The setup fee covers the design and build; the monthly fee covers hosting, care and support.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Managed hosting, domain management, SSL, backups, security, monthly content updates (2 on Signature, 5 on Growth, unlimited on Premium) and support. You never need to find a separate developer to keep your site running.

Why is there a monthly fee on top of the build?

A website is not a once-off job. Hosting, security, backups and regular updates are what keep it fast, safe and current. We bundle them into one predictable monthly price instead of billing you for every small request.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose Signature if you need a professional website that presents your business well. Choose Growth if you want the website to generate leads, with online booking, a blog and payment or CRM integration. Choose Premium for a fully bespoke build, e-commerce or custom features. Not sure? Book a free call and we will recommend one.

Can customers book or pay through the website?

Yes. Growth includes online booking with calendar integration and payment gateway or CRM integration using your own software. Premium adds e-commerce and client portal functionality.

Why do you only take ten clients at a time?

So every website gets real attention. We take three Signature, four Growth and three Premium clients at a time. When all ten slots are full, applications close until capacity opens up.
